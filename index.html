Options

ShapeSonic 2D

A shape is hidden on the screen. Find it by touch and listen. Headphones help.

  • Ambient hum: you are outside; it grows louder as you get closer.
  • Tone: a finger is inside the shape. It is loudest at the edge and fades toward the middle. Each finger plays its own note.
  • Bell: a finger is near a corner.

Touches on the left half of the screen are the left hand (left ear, lower pitch); the right half is the right hand. With a mouse, hold the button and drag.
